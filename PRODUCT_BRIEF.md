# MAYA product brief

## Product statement

MAYA is a local-first engineering workspace that helps an operator move from an objective to a
reviewed software outcome without losing context, control, or visibility.

## The problem

AI-assisted work can become difficult to trust when planning, changes, approvals, failures, and
proof are scattered across separate tools. A polished answer can look complete even when the
underlying work is unfinished or unclear.

## The product response

MAYA organizes the experience around four jobs:

1. **Frame the work** — establish the objective, context, boundaries, and desired outcome.
2. **Follow progress** — keep the current state and next action easy to understand.
3. **Review the result** — inspect changes and supporting evidence before acceptance.
4. **Recover deliberately** — surface blockers clearly and preserve a path forward.

## Experience design

The workbench is designed as a quiet operational environment:

- stable navigation for repeated workflows;
- one primary task narrative rather than competing dashboards;
- focused review and evidence surfaces;
- explicit states for work that needs attention;
- restrained motion and high-contrast typography;
- useful detail on demand without overwhelming the main flow.

## Product principles

### The operator remains authoritative

Automation may propose, organize, and execute bounded work. The operator retains the final decision
over important changes and outcomes.

### Completion should be understandable

A completed state should connect the objective, the resulting change, and the available evidence.
The interface should not ask the operator to trust an unexplained success message.

### Failure should preserve momentum

Blocked or unavailable work should explain what happened and make the next reasonable action clear.

### Privacy is part of the experience

Public materials use representative data. Internal workspaces, implementation details, provider
configuration, diagnostics, and operational history remain private.

## Current position

MAYA is an independently directed private product in active development. This public repository is
an interview-safe product overview rather than a source release or detailed implementation record.
